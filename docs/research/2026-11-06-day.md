## What worked

1. **Copying a stronger team's setup (profile grafts, B156).** Profile bots run our movement with another team's draft, build and bag. On engine 3 two of them beat our live bot, so their setup was worth more than our recent map fixes. Grafting meowest's setup onto v66 gave v67.
2. **Drafting per team size (B164-B172).** `groupgap.py` showed 04compass/04standing_stones losses; the cause was the three-pick draft, not movement. Arena on only the maps of one team size measures a roster change with little noise: v68 (3 picks), v69/v70 (4 and 5 picks, dropping Vesper).
3. **Re-checking old constants (B178-B184).** Recall thresholds (from v23/B121), spawn leave (a v5 tune), gold weight (v6) and group size (v16) were all stale. Each one-at-a-time sweep with `gate.py --mode gain` over all 128 maps found a gain.
4. **Tuning constants together (B193).** Once single sweeps stalled (B185-B191), `tune.py` over eight constants found a +64-gem step (`max_damage_share` +21% with smaller shifts elsewhere). Three more rounds (B194-B196) found nothing more.
5. **Four bodies per group fixed the Golem and elk-gem gaps (B189):** v71 → v73 against prof-aynir went 64% → 86% on seven Golem maps and 50% → 71% on seven elk maps.

## What failed

- **Cassi & Issac (B160, B162, B192).** The top 3 all draft the pair (58.7% wins when drafted). In our bot it dies 8-11 more times a game, even with linked-pair following, Vesper's fight rules and top-team builds: 31-42% vs v73. The pair dies to enemy bursts; it needs burst-aware positioning.
- **Forced grouping (B161).** Fight stats (`tools/fights.py`) show the top 3 keep 2.4 allies within 6 tiles vs our 1.8, but forcing pairs cost −323 margin. Part of their grouping is Cassi & Issac moving together.
- **Fight thresholds (B181, also B152 earlier).** A 1.4x engage edge cut deaths but stacked with B180 it gave flat gems, so it was dropped before submission.
- **Top-10 build grafts (B163), Power soak (B155), Ryth/HoR health soaks (B174, B176), Cassi-free Willow/Prism/Ren/Tavi swaps (B166, B171).**
