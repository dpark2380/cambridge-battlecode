# Path to 60% ranked (2026-10-28)

Goal from Daniel: find the changes most likely to lift the ranked win rate from about 50% to at least 60%. The live
bot is `bots/daniel/v18` (resubmitted 10:25Z after v22 went 50-61). We are about #284 of 802, rating 1611.

**Short answer.** We lose fights, not drafts. In ranked wins and losses alike, 16-17% of our contacts with an
enemy end in our death within 24 rounds. What changes in a loss is that the opponent stops dying: 16% of their
contacts in our wins, 5% in our losses, and 8% against the scrim panel. Our melee bodies barely hit champions.
Our Vendetta deals 1.8-1.9k champion damage a game to the opponent Vendetta's 10k. Four code-level causes in v18
explain most of this. Each was measured on real games:

1. `visible_champions` rates every champion's damage as `max(strength, focus)`. That overrates our Heart of Ruin
   and Vesper about 2x, and our focus builds more as they grow. Those two champions lose healthy 1v1s 26-37% of
   the time. The opponents' do 3-19%.
2. The low-health branch of `take_turn` walks a hurt body home without ever striking back. The gem duo (Heart of
   Ruin + Ryth) takes that branch at **75%** health whenever any enemy is in sight. Against the scrim panel, 46% of
   our deaths came while walking home (theirs: 19%). Ryth died that way 69% of the time, Heart of Ruin 57%.
3. Heart of Ruin plans as a **30-damage** body all game. The B41 dash damage never reaches `measure_damage`: 179
   of 190 status lines read `dmg=30`. So it farms lone gem clusters: only 11-12% of its clears have a monster in
   them, against 47-56% for opponent Heart of Ruins.
4. The gold squad (Vesper, Marcella, Vendetta) never steps toward an enemy. `fight_enemies` returns false when the
   target is out of reach, and the body goes back to farming.

The economy gap follows from the fights. In ranked losses, 39% of the r500 gold gap is kill gold (100 gold a kill).
Our gold ratio at r250 predicts the result better than anything else: 25% wins below 0.6x, 81% at 1.25x or more.
Two map-specific gaps add to it: we never take the wolf+gold group on 00galaxy, and we never take Golem groups on
02geodes. Fixing items 1-4 is the only route to 60% visible in this data. The gains below are **guesses** sized by
the gap each item closes.

The gem-duo role is set in `make_plan` (`draft.cpp:186-190`). It goes to the two highest `fight_score` picks,
Heart of Ruin (1.58) and Ryth (1.55), or Marcella when Ryth is not drafted. The stdout of `a4984561` confirms it:
`plan: heart_of_ruin gem_duo`, `plan: ryth gem_duo`.

**Why Heart of Ruin plans at 30.** `measure_damage` adds a spell only if it passes `ability.can_target_camps()`.
Heart of Ruin's dash is ground-targeted (`ability_dash`, `sdk/cpp/cambc.h:2823`). So the dash is most likely
skipped by that test, or `damage_amount()` throws and the `catch` drops it. Either way, 179 of 190 HoR status
lines in our 27 replays read `dmg=30`, which is base strength, and none exceed 54. HoR buys Focus
(`build_stat`), so that number never grows. A one-line debug print would show which of the two applies. B41's
casting half works: HoR camp damage per 1000 rounds rose from 5.0k (v17) to 8.1k (v18 ranked). Its planning half
never took effect.

**B56 (v22) pushed every body further onto clusters.** It priced monster-free groups at 120 health a cluster. The
share of clears with a monster fell from v18 to v22 for every champion: HoR 34% → 12%, Ryth 75% → 43%, Vendetta
71% → 54%, Vesper 57% → 46%. v8 had found that "planning with 120 over-values cluster groups"
(`bots/daniel/docs/v8/summary.md`). The panel does not show v18 beating v22, though: v18 went 10-50 there and v22
16-44. So B56 is not proven to be v22's ranked cause. v18 is live again, so no action is needed.

Items 1, 2 and 4 all change one decision: whether and how a body fights. They will overlap, so a guess for 1+2+3+4
together is **+6-10 points**. With 5-7 the total could reach the 60% target, but only if the fight changes hold on

### 1. Real damage in the fight check

- **Change.** In `visible_champions`, set `damage = strength + Σ(spell modifier x focus) / 8` per kit, instead of
  `max(strength, focus)`. Count the attack reset as `+strength/8`. For champions not in the table, fall back to
  `strength + focus / 8`. The table, from `docs/GameRules.md`: Ryth stun 1.0; Vesper and Vendetta reset only;
  Marcella reset + 2.0; HoR dash 1.5; Cassi 2.0; Prism, Stellar, Tavi, Willow, Ren and Varg from their kit
  entries. Use `ability.damage_amount()` for ourselves where the API allows it.
- **Why it should work.** Ordering our champions by how much the proxy overrates them (HoR ≈ Vesper > Ryth >
  Vendetta > Marcella) closely tracks their healthy-1v1 death rates in both data sets (table in §1). The bias grows
  with our Focus builds. A HoR with 20 Focus shards has a proxy of 300 and a real ~86. Opponents' Strength shards
  raise their real damage 1:1. That asymmetry is the B59 pattern.
- **Test.** First check the mechanism. Re-run `tools/fights.py` on 10-20 local replays against `bots/_var/pwr`
  (Power build) and expect HoR's and Vesper's 1v1 death rates to fall. Then run the scrim panel (baseline v18
  10-50, v22 16-44). The read that matters is the first 100 ranked games: contact death rate (16%), opponent
  contact death rate (5% in losses), and the win rate against ≥65% Strength opponents (47%). `gate.py --mode
  nonreg` against v18 only guards against crashes and blunders.

### 2. No walking home under attack

- **Change.** In the leave branch (`bot.cpp:1133-1137`), when an enemy champion is within 2 tiles, do what B37's
  outmatched branch already does: strike the weakest enemy in reach before stepping (ranged), or after stepping
  (melee). Step toward the nearest ally within 12 tiles, not toward spawn. Use `duo_recall_health` (0.7487) only
  while carrying gems, or drop it to `carrier_recall_health` (0.5). B41b, HoR off the duo, raced at 56% vs v17
  (`bots/daniel/CHANGELOG.md`, v18 entry) but was never applied.
- **Why.** The duo's 75% line fires whenever any enemy is in vision. A walking body cannot outrun a same-speed
  chaser: melee chasers walk and hit, and Vendetta and HoR also dash. The body takes free hits for 6-8 rounds, as
  in the `0fb79e5a` HoR example. HoR's passive also adds up to +50% damage at low health, which fleeing throws away.
- **Caveat.** In the 9 ranked v22 losses, only 24% of our deaths came while walking home, against 37% of theirs.
  The flight pattern is strongest against the stronger panel teams. The low-health escape gap (36% vs 21% died)
  holds in both sets.
- **Test.** The mechanism check is "deaths while walking home" and the low-health death rate in `tools/fights.py`.
  Then run the panel and the first 100 ranked games, watching champion deaths per game (v22 losses: 14.8).

### 3. Heart of Ruin's planning damage

- **Change.** In `measure_damage`, add `damage_amount() / max_cooldown` for a dash with `dash_modifier > 0`, with
  no `can_target_camps()` test. Print the result once to confirm about 49 at base focus. Optionally scale it by the
  expected targets hit (B41 note: 1.5).
- **Why.** At 30, HoR fails `survivable()` (`bot.cpp:220`) on most monster groups. It then farms clusters (geodes:
  nine 7-gem bags in `9e2d57d3`) or waits for help. It earns 120-200 gold by r500 against the opponent HoR's
  246-340, stays the weakest body, and dies the most on the panel: 183-185 deaths in losses.
- **Risk.** More solo monster groups means more camp damage. The trips-home term in `choose_target` already
  charges for it.
- **Test.** Check that the stdout reads `dmg≈49`. Then check HoR's monster-group share of clears (target 40%+) and
  HoR gold at r500 in the panel summaries. Race vs v18 locally for safety; this one is local-testable because it
  changes farming.

### 4. Join a fight already in progress

- **Change.** In `fight_enemies`, when the check passes, a gold-squad melee body steps toward the weakest enemy
  within `engage_radius` and strikes, as the duo does. When the check fails, a ranged body with a melee enemy
  adjacent steps away first, and uses its attack reset to still shoot (Vesper slot 0, `docs/GameRules.md:520`).
- **Bound.** This is not B46 hunting (43%), which crossed the map for carriers. It only closes the 2-4 tiles to an
  enemy already in a fight we judged even. It depends on item 1, or it will commit to the same misjudged fights.

## 5. How to test against real opponents

- **The panel first.** `tools/scrim.py panel` gives 60 games against 6 teams and 10 maps, a fixed set across
  versions. The baselines are v18 at 10-50 and v22 at 16-44. One standard error over 60 games is about 6 points,
  so a +10-point change shows as about 22-26 wins. Opponents may update their bots between panels: compare v18 and
  the candidate in panels run close together.
- **Mechanism metrics, not only W-L.** Download about 10 panel replays per version (`cambc matches download <id>
  -o …`, ~8 s each) and run `node tools/replay/analyze.mjs` and `tools/fights.py`. Each fix predicts one number:
  - item 1: 1v1 deaths of HoR and Vesper;
  - item 2: deaths while walking home;
  - item 3: HoR `dmg=` and its monster-group share;
  - item 4: Vendetta adjacency and champion damage.
- **Then ranked.** The first ~100 games after submission. Rating-matched pairing pulls a long ranked average toward
  50% (backlog T4), so 60% shows only while the bot climbs.
- **Locally,** use `bots/_var/pwr` and `bots/simon/hunter-v0` as stand-ins that chase. Self-play and v12/Jame
  gates do not punish fleeing or a misjudged fight, as B14, B28 and B59 show.
